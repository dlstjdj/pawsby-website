Delete your account

This erases the records we hold about you on our servers. We'll email a six-digit code first so nobody else can do this to your account.

What gets erased

  • Your subscription record and email
  • Your account and device identifiers
  • Every encrypted chat row we hold for you
  • Your connected device list

What this doesn't do

  • Chat history on your own computer stays — delete it in Settings → Chat
  • Billing isn't cancelled. Cancel the subscription first, or email us to do both
  • Invoices we must keep for tax law are retained

We'll send a code that expires in 5 minutes. Nothing is deleted yet.
