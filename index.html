Questions

Before you ask

Which AI does it use?

Standard runs on GPT-4o mini, Pro on Claude Haiku 4.5. We swap these for better models as they appear — the plan is the promise, not a particular vendor. Prefer your own? Any paid plan lets you plug in your own API key.

Can it read my screen or files?

No. It sees what you type and, if you connect a calendar, your upcoming events. Nothing else.

What happens if I cancel?

You keep everything until the period you paid for ends, then the cat drops back to the free plan and keeps walking. Your chat history stays on your machine.

Is my conversation private?

History lives in an encrypted database on your computer. Sync is off unless you turn it on, and when it's on your messages are encrypted on this device first, so we store ciphertext rather than readable text. Full details.
