Features

Company, not another chat window

Everything runs on your machine. The cat is the interface.

It actually lives there

Walks the floor, sits on window edges, gets thrown and lands on its feet. Not a floating widget.

Talks back

Give it a name, a personality, and its own instructions. It remembers the conversation and looks things up when it needs to.

Meeting nudges

Connect Google or Outlook Calendar. Your cat warns you before a meeting instead of a system banner you'll ignore.

Local by default

Chat history stays in an encrypted database on your device. Sync is opt-in, end-to-end encrypted, and the server only ever holds ciphertext.

Focus mode

Send it to a corner to sleep — on a schedule, or whenever you need the screen to yourself.

Commands and dark mode

Type /sleep, /play, /eat. Three chat layouts, light and dark, English, 한국어 and 日本語.

Four breeds, every plan
